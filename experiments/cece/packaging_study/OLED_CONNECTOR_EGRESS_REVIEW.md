# Adafruit 4741 OLED connector egress in the V3 heart frame

**Status: display-body solid intersection checks pass, but there is no usable modeled cable exit.** This blocks a claim that the OLED can be wired in the V3 assembly.

The [official Adafruit 4741 STEP model](reference/adafruit_4741.step) and [Eagle board files](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit-Grayscale-1.5-inch-128x128-OLED-PCB) identify two JST-SH/STEMMA QT connectors at the **long-side** edges of the PCB, plus a rear through-hole row. The placement in [`build_oled_window_v3.py`](build_oled_window_v3.py) rotates PCB native X into robot Z. Thus the two JST-SH receptacles face the installed **top and bottom** of the OLED, behind the heart frame. In [`generate_oled_window_v3_mesh.py`](generate_oled_window_v3_mesh.py), the frame pocket is 48 × 36.56 mm in robot X/Z. The installed board spans approximately ±23.495 mm X and ±17.78 mm Z, leaving only **0.505 mm** beyond each board side edge to the frame pocket wall. This is a PCB clearance, not a connector/cable clearance. The receptacle mouth is immediately inside that edge and a mated JST-SH housing plus cable must project by several millimetres; neither top nor bottom port can be mated in this frame. A nominal zero-intersection test against the bare OLED CAD does not test the mating plug.

## Minimal-wire, through-hole route to develop

The board's native rear row `JP2` has **eight 2.54 mm-pitch plated through holes**, in order GND, VIN, 3Vo, CS, Rst, A0/DC, Clk, Data (the [official Adafruit Eagle board source](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit-Grayscale-1.5-inch-128x128-OLED-PCB)). Adafruit says the display ships in I2C mode; the required connections for basic I2C are GND, VIN, Clk/SCL and Data/SDA, with Rst available if needed. VIN accepts 3–5 V. [Adafruit pinout](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-grayscale-1-5-128x128-oled-display/pinouts). A straight or right-angle **1×8 through-hole header** soldered on the OLED, with one 1×8 female housing and only the needed four or five wires crimped, would avoid soldering surface-mount parts and permit unplugging the face. Keep all eight positions in one housing so GND/VIN/SCL/SDA cannot shift by one pin, and provide a positive orientation key/retainer in the final design: a bare pin header and ordinary female housing are reversible. Do **not** assume a 4-position female shell at one end of the 8-pin row is keyed or safe.

This route is **not yet validated** either: the native header row at Y ≈39.37 mm transforms to robot X ≈+20.96 mm, while the current body's central 30.2 mm window ends at robot X = +18.2 mm. Depending on header orientation and wire bend, a header or mating housing can hit the body. Model the exact header and chosen female housing behind the PCB, cut a rear service recess/channel as necessary, retain the PCB, and re-run assembled solid and cable clearance checks. Alternatively, design a reinforced top/bottom notch around one JST-SH lead using the actual selected cable's mated plug and bend envelope. Do not print the current heart frame as a final connected assembly.

### V4 passage candidate (29 October 2026)

[`Cece_Body_4741_HeaderRouteV4_CANDIDATE.stl`](Cece_Body_4741_HeaderRouteV4_CANDIDATE.stl) adds a narrow passage to the newer charger-post body at world X = 17.71–24.21, Y = −42…−16.75, Z = −29.45…−5.95 mm. The STL reloads as one watertight solid. [`oled_connector_probe_results.json`](oled_connector_probe_results.json) records exact FreeCAD intersection tests: a **provisional** 5 × 17 × 22 mm 1×8 rear connector envelope centred at `(20.96, −26, −17.7)` and two 4–6 mm cable-bundle corridor boxes have zero overlap with the V4 body, head, heart frame, official OLED CAD, hip-servo proxy, battery or UBEC envelopes. The same connector box intersects the earlier body by 259.416 mm³, explaining the new passage. The upper/bottom built-in JST-SH plug proxies still clash with the unmodified body/head or frame, so this is a PTH route only.

The 1×8 housing dimensions and wire bend are **design envelopes**, not a selected product model. The actual header, keyed housing, pin orientation, solder joints, board retainer, cable strain relief and circuit continuity must be checked on the real display before this becomes a build-ready route. The front heart frame hides the passage but currently has no proven attachment or display retainer.

[`oled_mount_probe_results.json`](oled_mount_probe_results.json) tests the official board's nominal four mounting holes at 41.91 × 30.48 mm installed pitch. Four Ø2.2 mm screw-axis probes pass through the official OLED STEP holes with zero solid intersection. They do **not** all pass through material in the current heart silhouette: the lower two axes have no frame material at those positions, while the upper two only cross a few cubic millimetres of frame. A direct four-screw frame/PCB stack therefore needs new mounting ears or a different retainer; the present heart frame cannot be treated as screw-mounted merely because the PCB has holes.

The [Adafruit product pinout](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-grayscale-1-5-128x128-oled-display/pinouts) confirms both side ports are wired in parallel. JST's [SH-series catalog](https://www.jst.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/eSH-new.pdf) identifies the mating housing family; it does not provide a Cece-specific cable bend radius. Check actual cable, header and frame together before claiming fit.
