# Cece mechanical assembly joint audit — 29 October 2026

**Decision: NO-GO for a complete Cece print or a claim that the robot clicks together.** Several major joints are only aligned in CAD, with no positive retainer. The purchased SG90 horn, actual printed tolerances, speaker plug and electronic mounts have not been physically checked. Zero mesh intersection is a clearance result, not an assembly or strength result.

## Exact scope and evidence

The neutral assembly in `../balance_study/latest_inputs.json` uses the V4 display-header body, V3 heart frame, rear speaker head/pod, UC2 rear cassette, 45° passive arms, antenna, SG90 ankle V2 legs and wide feet. The preferred PiicoDev cable hood is `servo_plug_check/Cece_head_vertical_driver_hood_V2_CANDIDATE.stl`. A separate combined side-speaker/faceplate-fastener trial is in `speaker_pod/side_route_v3/`; it derives from that V2 hood. The central plug-passage V2 in `speaker_pod/plug_passage_v2/` is rejected for the current ESP location.

`packaging_results.json`, the charger and speaker pod `mesh_validation.json` reports, `ankle_v2/freecad_fit_results.json`, and `balance_study/wide_foot/wide_foot_checks.json` establish closed mesh / static non-interference at their stated poses and proxies. They do not demonstrate friction, snap retention, fastener pull-out, part insertion order or real electronic connector clearance. The full packaging scene uses provisional hardware boxes; its earlier horizontal driver placement had plug/head clashes, while the newer hood/vertical driver is still a candidate with unmeasured plugs.

Keep LiPo power disconnected during mechanical fitting.

## Minimal physical test coupon sequence

1. **One ankle set already modeled:** print one V2 leg, its matching wide foot, one washer and optional thin shims. Fit the actual SG90 and stock horn; tie the case, install correct horn and washer screws, then hand-swing and run slow ±20° on a limited 5 V supply. Reject any binding, horn slip, axial pull-out, cracked pivot or case motion.
2. **One pilot-hole strip:** print Ø1.6, Ø1.8 and Ø2.5 mm pilots in the same printer orientation and wall thickness as the ankle peg, pod and cassette posts. Install the actual M2×8, M2×5 and M3×10 screws at controlled hand torque; reject cracked, stripped or bottomed posts. This strip does not replace pull tests on the real geometry.
3. **Head/body and one arm pin coupon:** use the [separate four-screw joint candidate](head_body_joint/README.md) for a full mating rim band test (a small arc cannot prove the whole joint), and print one full side-pin/arm eye. Check M3 pilot grip and 0.3 mm tongue insertion; cycle the arm ten times without cracking. Weigh the actual head+pod and test the finished head joint with a measured load at least several times that weight, chosen after the final mass is known.
4. **Front electronics coupon:** print a slice containing both ultrasonic eye holes, the V3 faceplate screw bosses/holes and at least one 5 mm and one 3 mm LED hole. Fit the actual M2 screws and purchased HC-SR04 PCB/cans and LEDs; confirm faceplate pull-off resistance, can/lead clearance and no stress on solder joints. The sensor and LEDs still need their own retainers.
5. **Screen and speaker cable coupon:** print the V4 window/heart frame region with its final PCB mounts and chosen 1×8 keyed connector, plus the V3 **side** speaker passage with the actual preattached plug and installed ESP/amp stand-ins. Verify insert/remove order, service access, cable bend and strain relief before making a full head/body print. The earlier V2 central passage failed against the ESP envelope; V3 clears a provisional plug box but has not been physically fitted.

Passing these coupons is necessary but still does not prove gait, center of mass, servo torque, USB charging safety or long-term printed-part fatigue. After the retention redesigns, build one unpowered complete assembly and then conduct limited powered movement tests with the actual parts.
