# Cece whole-robot CAD packaging study — 29 October 2026

**Status: engineering candidate, not an approved full print or powered build.** This study uses the four SG90 servos bought from Rapid Robotics, the ordered Dualsky 550 mAh 2S LiPo and Hobbywing 5 A UBEC, and the selected Core electronics. The 2S USB-C balance charger is an **unpurchased** ISDT UC2 candidate. The exact ESP32-C3 SuperMini variant, battery connectors, actual servo accessories and several module dimensions remain unmeasured.

The assembled FreeCAD scene is [`Cece_Electronics_Packaging_Study.FCStd`](Cece_Electronics_Packaging_Study.FCStd). [`layout_check.py`](layout_check.py) regenerates it and the [machine-readable fit results](packaging_results.json) using FreeCAD 1.1. It places the official [Adafruit 4741 STEP](reference/adafruit_4741.step), a partial PiicoDev servo-driver STEP rebuilt from the vendor's KiCad files, the final candidate printed shells, and explicitly sized clearance envelopes for modules, servos, selected plugs and initial cable passages. A zero-volume intersection of these shapes is a **nominal static geometry result**, not a physical fit or working-gait result.

## What the CAD establishes

- The first battery position collided with both hip-servo case proxies by **3,164.8 mm³ each**. The present battery clearance box is rotated to `(0, +3, −24)` mm; UBEC clearance box is shifted to `(+2.3, 0, −8)` mm. Their current candidate boxes clear the hip proxies and printed shell. See [hip study](hip_servo_probe_results.json).
- The old horizontal servo-driver orientation put all four mated plug proxies into the head roof. The revised **vertical front-facing** driver at `(+1, +11.8, +13)` mm, four modeled plugs, vertical ESP at `(0, +24, +10)` mm and the V2 roof hood have zero nominal overlap with the combined V3 head/body, antenna and selected electronic envelopes. The partial driver STEP has **2.05 mm** to the ESP envelope. See [driver study](servo_plug_check/README.md).
- The official Adafruit 4741 model clears the revised body and heart frame. The active window has nominal margins **left 2.244, right 1.096, bottom 1.797, top 1.543 mm**. The board's built-in STEMMA QT sockets cannot accept a plug inside this frame. The V4 body adds a **provisional** zero-clash rear through-hole header passage; the actual keyed housing and display retainer have not been selected. See [OLED cable review](OLED_CONNECTOR_EGRESS_REVIEW.md).
- The candidate UC2 rear tray and Ø28 mm V3 speaker pod are each watertight STL solids and have zero static structural overlap with the body/head. A provisional 8 × 7 × 16 mm speaker-plug box clears the V3 side passage and ESP, and two M2 bosses now give the faceplate a positive attachment candidate. Their actual USB-C, XH and speaker plugs, lead bend into the amp, screw pull-out and sensor/LED retention are still unverified. See [charger cassette](charger_cassette/README.md) and [V3 speaker/face study](speaker_pod/side_route_v3/README.md).
- The ankle V2 printed joint and servo-case proxies clear the revised wide feet at neutral and in sampled ±45° sweeps. The real SG90 case, supplied horns, screws, cable ties, print shrink and bearing strength still need a one-joint physical test. See [ankle V2](../ankle_v2/README.md).

The current [static balance analysis](../balance_study/LATEST_BALANCE.md) uses exact STL volume/centroid and the actual wider-sole polygons. Its analytical 20% infill estimate is **153.077 g** of PLA, with **251.077 g** including the battery, UBEC, four SG90s and 6 g UC2 candidate, excluding several boards, the speaker, wiring and screws. With that stated incomplete payload, the neutral two-foot COM has a **+25.051 mm ideal support margin**. It is about **4 mm outside either one-foot sole**; reaching a chosen 5 mm one-foot reserve needs about **9 mm lateral COM shift**. The optional 12 g speaker sensitivity reduces the two-foot margin to **+23.329 mm**. These figures are not slicer estimates or a demonstrated walking gait.

## Open build gates

1. **Actual plugs and mounting:** measure the delivered SG90 case/horn, PiicoDev USB-C/JST and servo-plug grips, OLED header/housing, speaker connector and battery plugs. Make a one-joint print and dry-fit. The partial PiicoDev STEP omits both USB-C and JST sockets; modeled service spaces are conservative placeholders.
2. **Charging and power:** the UBEC is not a charger. The proposed UC2 requires a matching 2S XH balance lead, an accessible main power OFF switch, protected wiring, strain relief and verification of USB-C plug access. The battery main plug may be a 3 A JST RCY type, potentially limiting current below the UBEC's 5 A output rating. See [power draft](CHARGE_POWER_DRAFT.md) and [charge interface review](CHARGING_INTERFACE_REVIEW.md).
3. **Fastening and strength:** verify head-to-body, heart/display, face LEDs, driver/ESP, amp/mic, arms, charger cassette, speaker pod, servo clamps/horns and screws. Printed pilots and snap features need test coupons; current exact non-intersection is not a load or drop test.
   A separate [four-screw head/body joint trial](head_body_joint/README.md) has clear CAD screw paths, but its 0.3 mm tongue gap and 0.5 mm pilot wall need a physical print and pull-out check. It is outside the 17-STL candidate archive.
4. **Electrical/firmware:** confirm the actual ESP32-C3 SuperMini pinout and power-backfeed behavior, finish the through-hole wiring schematic and LED drive, measure four-servo current/5 V sag, then test audio/display/sensor/charge and low-voltage behavior. The [connection schedule](../electronics/ADAFRUIT_CONNECTION_SCHEDULE_DRAFT.md) is still a draft.
5. **Walking:** weigh actual prints and electronics, re-run COM through a measured gait, then run restrained movement on a level surface. The old MuJoCo animation uses proxy geometry and is not a movement validation.

## Regenerate the checks

From the repository root, use FreeCAD 1.1's bundled Python for [`layout_check.py`](layout_check.py). The OLED/body mesh generators use Python with `trimesh` and `manifold3d`; the focused cassette, speaker, ankle and driver folders have their own scripts and reports. [`latest_balance.py`](../balance_study/latest_balance.py) runs with standard Python 3 and records source STL hashes. Keep the recorded component dimensions with the result: replacing any board, plug, battery or print profile invalidates the corresponding clearance or mass conclusion.
