# Current Cece gait run

The current heart V4 and head without the LED mouth were used for visual meshes and updated solid-PLA mass properties. The dynamics model remains an idealized Bullet model with approximate geometry and hardware; it cannot prove the actual robot will walk. `bullet_current_cece_video.mp4` is a 4-half-step simulation recording, not a physical robot. The 10-half-step nominal and 480 Hz runs completed 10 lift-and-land events without a simulated fall, with approximately 84.9 mm and 84.1 mm of forward displacement, respectively. The heart clears leg/foot geometry in 800 discrete samples on the nominal trajectory. Simulation does not include actual horns, screws, wires, gearbox compliance, physical friction, servo currents or battery voltage sag. The head and heart do not use their full mesh as physical collision geometry in Bullet. The printed robot's actual mass, fit and walking need measurement and testing.
