# Optional widened feet for Cece ankle V2

The baseline feet leave a 30 mm gap between inner sole edges. This candidate adds a 5.02 mm thick inward extension, fused to each current ankle V2 foot, while keeping the SG90 horn mount and bearing geometry unchanged. At neutral pose the new inner edges are x = ±4 mm, leaving an **8 mm gap**. The extension has 5 mm chamfered inner corners in plan view.

`build_wide_feet.py` uses FreeCAD 1.1 exact solids and exports the two printable STLs plus `Cece_Wider_Feet_Option.FCStd`. Each output is one valid watertight solid. `wide_foot_checks.json` records zero neutral foot-to-foot intersection and zero intersections in sampled ±45° ankle sweeps (5° steps) for revised leg, assumed SG90 body/turret/shaft and bearing washer. It also checks one foot rotated about its ankle axis while the other remains flat at the same sample angles; no mesh overlap was found. This is not a combined hip/ankle walking trajectory or a physical slip/load test.

`analyze_wide_support.py` uses exact sole vertices and the documented shell assembly transforms. At nominal 100%-solid PLA density 1.24 g/cm³, the wider shells total 137.12 g, about 7.37 g more than baseline, and their shell-only COM height is 50.09 mm above the floor. A centred shell COM remains **4 mm outside** either single-foot support area; it needs about **9 mm** lateral shift for the declared 5 mm reserve, compared with about **20 mm** for the baseline soles. This is an improvement, not a proof that the servo gait can make that shift. The actual battery, electronics, printed infill and component placements will change full mass and COM.

Print only as a test variant until a full-body fit and gait/ground-contact assessment is complete. These extensions should not be treated as a final solution if the actual foot touches another part, tilts onto an edge, or scrapes the floor during stepping. The resting 8 mm gap may be reduced by print elephant foot or assembly error; check the real clearance. There are no new servos or electronics in this option.
