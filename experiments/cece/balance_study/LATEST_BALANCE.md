# Cece current candidate: neutral static balance

Run `python experiments/cece/balance_study/latest_balance.py` from the repository root or any directory. It reads `latest_inputs.json` and writes `latest_balance_results.json`. The report records the SHA-256 of every STL used. It includes the current **V4 OLED-header-route body** with charger posts and balance-lead slot, the V3 heart frame, charger tray/bezel, the **V3 combined head with V2 servo-driver hood and enlarged side speaker route**, V3 M2-retained faceplate and side-notched speaker pod, speaker grille, ankle V2 legs, **wider** feet and the other existing Cece decorative parts. The V4 body, V3 frame, faceplate and cassette/pod files are already in assembled coordinates; only the antenna and two arms need the translations listed in the input. The V4 body removes 343.75 mm³ from the prior cassette body for a provisional rear OLED header passage; the header, female housing and cable masses are still unknown.

The charger is modeled at `(0, 63.3, -24)` mm with its manufacturer-listed 6 g mass. The purchased battery and UBEC use 35 g and 21 g seller masses at candidate `(0, 3, -24)` and `(2.3, 0, -8)` mm. Four SG90s use 9 g each at the ankle CAD case centres and inferred hip case centres. These placements are **provisional centres**, not measured mass centroids. The speaker is centered at `(0, 44.7, 17)` mm. SparkFun and Core publish its 28 mm diameter and thickness but no mass; a separate **12 g sensitivity case** uses a reseller's listing and is not treated as a measured speaker weight. The other electronic boards and wiring have no verified mass and are excluded from totals. Their candidate positions and the effect of adding 10 g at each position are in `latest_balance_results.json`.

## Printed-mass models

- **Solid STL plastic:** every closed STL volume filled with PLA at an assumed 1.24 g/cm³. This is an upper bound for the *modeled plastic volume* before supports, brims, adhesives and extra mounts.
- **Nominal 20% infill:** three 0.4 mm walls are represented by a 1.2 mm equivalent outer skin. For each STL, estimated fully solid skin fraction is `min(1, surface area × 1.2 mm / solid volume)`; the remainder gets 20% material. The printed part keeps its solid-volume centroid. The formula is a first-order geometry estimate, **not a Bambu Studio or other slicer result**. Thin modeled shells become effectively solid under this model; the two feet and arms account for most of the mass reduction. Wall overlap, top/bottom layers, infill pattern and print supports are only approximated.

The V3 combined head, faceplate and side-notched speaker pod add **632.192 mm³ of plastic, or 0.784 g at solid PLA density**, relative to the previous V2-hood head, draft faceplate and original pod together. The skin model treats these thin parts as fully solid, so it adds the same 0.784 g in the nominal 20% case. The V3 head itself has 561.407 mm³ more plastic than the standalone V2-hood head; the revised faceplate removes 30.066 mm³ and the pod adds 100.851 mm³.

The support polygon is formed from the **actual lowest vertices** of both wider-foot STLs on a level floor. Positive margin means the projected COM lies inside the ideal support polygon. In this neutral pose, the modeled mass is inside the two-foot support area but outside **either single planted foot** by about 4 mm. To reach a chosen 5 mm one-foot reserve, the COM must shift laterally about 9 mm; this is a mass-centre translation, not a programmed hip/ankle angle. A 5 mm reserve is a design comparison threshold rather than a tested safe factor. The 12 g speaker sensitivity moves the COM rearward and upward and reduces the two-foot margin by about 1.7 mm. The nominal 20% model reduces plastic mass by about 12.1 g because the body, head, trays and other small features are already thin shells.

For a simple rigid-body lateral acceleration estimate, the tip boundary is `a = g × support margin / COM height`. The current neutral **single-foot** margins are negative, so the available outward acceleration is **0 m/s²** in that pose: it must first shift its COM into the planted sole. After shifting to the illustrative **5 mm reserve**, the formula gives **0.84 m/s²** for the solid-plastic/listed-hardware case and **0.78 m/s²** for the nominal-print/12 g speaker sensitivity. These are idealized quasi-static limits. Joint motion changes the COM and angular momentum; floor friction, a nonflat sole, compliant plastic and impact can cause a fall below these numbers. They are not validated walking speeds or safe control limits.

## What this result does not establish

The ESP32-C3, PiicoDev driver, OLED, HC-SR04, microphone, amplifier, LEDs, resistors, switches, connectors, wires, screws, floor pads and print supports lack weighed masses in this calculation. The listed board positions are packaging-envelope centres, not measured mass centroids. The ultrasonic board and OLED are forward of the COM; the speaker, ESP and audio boards are rearward. Their eventual measured masses will change the balance. The report gives a **10 g position sensitivity** for each module with a known candidate centre, without implying that any module actually weighs 10 g.

The charger cassette and speaker pod mesh checks establish nominal solid non-intersection for the tested parts. The [V4 OLED header route](../packaging_study/oled_header_route_v4_mesh_results.json) creates a provisional passage from the display's rear through-hole row; the actual header/housing, orientation key, cable bend and frame retention remain unverified. The [V3 head, faceplate and speaker side route](../packaging_study/speaker_pod/side_route_v3/exact_driver_check.json) pass modeled plug-proxy and CAD clash checks, but the purchased PiicoDev JST-SH plug, speaker wire/plug and bend radii have not been physically measured. The V3 faceplate M2 pilot grip, screw length, sensor and LED retention also require a trial print. The UC2 XH balance lead and USB-C plug offsets, insertion clearance, cable strain relief and charge isolation still require a physical test. Servo cable bend, horn/screw fit, printed joint strength, floor friction, running torque/current, contact pressure and a jointed walking gait are unverified. The existing MuJoCo animation is a visual proxy, not a gait validation.

The reported mass is **not a final robot weight or payload rating**. Slice the exact STL set with the chosen PLA profile, weigh the printed parts and components, update the input masses and actual mounted centres, then recompute COM through every intended gait pose and perform a restrained physical walking test.
